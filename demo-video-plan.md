# Lofi Flurry Dash — Demo Video Plan

Target: 60-90 seconds, landscape, screen recording of the live demo. The hackathon form asks for a demo video; keep it short and show the game working, not a slide deck.

## Recording

- Easiest: open the live demo on a phone or laptop, screen-record in landscape while playing.
  - Live demo: https://dzulqarnainfaisal.github.io/lofi-flurry-dash/
  - Phone: turn on screen recording, open the demo in Chrome, play in landscape fullscreen.
  - Laptop: OBS or the built-in screen recorder, record the browser window.
- Record at least 3 short runs so there is good gameplay to cut from. Aim for one run that reaches Zone 2 (500 m) to show the snowball.

## Shot list (60-90 s cut)

1. **0-5 s — Title.** Game title screen, "LOFI FLURRY DASH", Lofi on the snowy slope. No narration needed, let the chiptune music play.
2. **5-20 s — Zone 1 gameplay.** Jump rocks and ice spikes, grab snowflakes, show stamina draining and refilling. Show the score and distance climbing.
3. **20-35 s — Zone 2.** Ice Cavern background change, low hanging ice (slide under it), first rolling snowball. Show the Speed Burst crystal if one appears.
4. **35-50 s — Zone 3 / late run.** Avalanche Peak, fastest speed, a golden snowflake (+250). Show a game over with the score and the local leaderboard name entry.
5. **50-65 s — Leaderboard + Sui.** Scroll to the leaderboard section, show the local Top 10, then the "On-chain Leaderboard (Sui)" section. Say or caption: "Scores can be submitted on-chain with a Sui wallet."
6. **65-75 s — Close.** Title screen again + the demo link on screen: dzulqarnainfaisal.github.io/lofi-flurry-dash

## Narration / captions (keep it simple)

- On screen text is enough; no voiceover required.
- If adding captions: "A 2D endless runner starring Lofi the Yeti. Built for CLAY Hackathon III on Sui. Play free, no wallet needed."

## Checklist before uploading

- [ ] Video is landscape, game is fully visible, no WhatsApp header or phone bars in frame (record in Chrome, not in the WhatsApp webview).
- [ ] Sound on (chiptune music) or add it in editing.
- [ ] Under 5 minutes (aim 60-90 s).
- [ ] Upload to public YouTube (unlisted is fine if the form accepts a link, public is safer).
- [ ] Put the YouTube link + demo link + repo link in the hackathon form.
