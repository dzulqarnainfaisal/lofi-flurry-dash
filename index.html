      Three zones, three different worlds

      The run is endless, but the world changes as you go: every zone has its own sky, scenery, and weather.

      There are no fixed levels to finish. You run until Lofi's 3 lives are gone, and the mountain transforms around you as the distance climbs. Watch the zone tag in the top-left of the game: when it flips, the whole background cross-fades into the next world.

      Zone 1 · Snowy Slope (0-500 m)Bright blue morning sky, soft sun, pine trees, and gentle white hills. The pace starts slow and easy here: rocks, ice spikes, hanging ice, and snowmen while you find your rhythm. No rolling snowballs yet.
      Zone 2 · Ice Cavern (500-1,500 m)The light drops into a deep teal cave: stalactites hang overhead, glowing ice crystals line the walls, and the snow turns pale blue. Rolling snowballs join the hazards here, so the run starts to bite.
      Zone 3 · Avalanche Peak (1,500 m+)A stormy purple dusk with jagged black peaks, driving snow, and wind streaks. Everything is faster here, and obstacles come thick and fast.

      Lofi's stats are real mechanics

      On Lofi's official character art, all three stats read 99.9: Stamina, Speed, and Intelligence. In this game none of them are decoration. Each one is a live system while you run.

      STAMINA
      99.9

      Starts at 99.9 and drains about 1 per second while running. Snowflakes restore +15. Hit 0 and Lofi runs at half speed with shorter jumps until stamina climbs back above 30.

      SPEED
      99.9

      The run starts at an easy jog and speeds up gradually with distance: gentle through Snowy Slope, quicker in Ice Cavern, fastest on Avalanche Peak. Grab a blue ice crystal for a Speed Burst x2 for 3 seconds, and distance points count double during the burst.

      INTELLIGENCE
      99.9

      Golden snowflakes are the brains bonus: +250 points each. Collect 5 gold flakes in one run to unlock the Genius Yeti +500 point bonus.

      RockSits on the ground. Jump it.

      Meet Lofi

      Lofi is a tall yeti with shaggy white fur, a blue face, and blue hands and feet. His card stats are Stamina 99.9, Speed 99.9, Intelligence 99.9, and all three drive the gameplay above.

      The in-game Lofi is the same logo-style yeti, running on a real 3-frame cycle so his legs stride faster as he speeds up, with a dedicated low slide pose and a stretched jump pose, plus a little snow spray kicked up as he runs.
